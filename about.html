About Me

My name is Jorge Gonzalez, and I am a Mexico residency and visa specialist with over 20 years of experience helping individuals and families obtain Temporary and Permanent Mexico Residency.

Since 2002, I have operated my business from San Diego, California, assisting clients throughout the United States with Mexico residency visa applications and document preparation. I have been a Better Business Bureau (BBB) member since 2010, reflecting my long-standing commitment to professionalism and client trust.

I hold dual citizenship in Mexico and the United States, which gives me a clear understanding of how Mexican immigration procedures work on both sides of the border.

Expertise in Mexico Residency

As a bilingual Mexico residency facilitator, fluent in Spanish and English, I help clients navigate the often complex requirements for:

  • Temporary Mexico Residency visas and cards
  • Permanent Mexico Residency visas and cards
  • Mexico residency document preparation and explanation

Because most official Mexico immigration documents are in Spanish, I ensure that every instruction, form, and requirement is clearly explained before submission. This helps clients avoid delays, mistakes, and unnecessary repeat visits.

Services Offered

  • Temporary Mexico Residency Visa and Card Applications
  • Permanent Mexico Residency Visa and Card Applications
  • Mexico Residency Document Translation and Explanation
  • Canje Process Guidance (exchange of visa for residency card in Mexico).

All services focus on document organization, accuracy, and preparation prior to your consulate or INM appointment.

​Why Choose My Services

  • Experience: Over two decades of focused experience in Mexico residency services
  • Bilingual Advantage: Clear communication in both English and Spanish
  • Exclusive Focus: Specializing only in Mexico residency, not general immigration
  • Personal Support: Direct guidance from start to finish

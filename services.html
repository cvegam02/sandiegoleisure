Canje from San Diego

Need Assistance with the CANJE Process?

Our service is available FROM San Diego, California. Enjoy a streamlined, half-day process!

[Click here for more details]

San Diego Leisure is a California-based company that helps clients with U.S. visa renewals in Mexico and with Mexico residency applications and related logistics.

1. Tijuana Visa Stamping & Consular Trip Packages

  • Consular trip support for renewing U.S. visas at the U.S. Consulate in Tijuana.
  • Includes hotel lodging, transportation, and assistance with appointments and logistics.
  • Packages are structured (e.g., 3 days / 2 nights) and start/end at San Diego airport or local hotels.

2. MRV (U.S. Visa Fee) Payment Service

San Diego Leisure can make your MRV payment on your behalf at a Mexican bank for scheduling U.S. visa appointments.

Fees: Typically $90 for the first person plus additional amounts for others in the family or group.

  • Receipts mailed via priority mail.
  • Works with consulates across Mexico (Tijuana, Matamoros, Nogales, Cd. Juarez, Mexico City, and more).

How it works:

Complete your DS-160 form. Generate payment instructions. Email instructions to Jorge (contact). Pay MRV + service fee. Payment processed at Mexico bank. You receive scanned receipt and then original by mail. Use the receipt to schedule your ASC & visa interviews.

  1. Complete your DS-160 form.
  2. Generate payment instructions.
  3. Email instructions to Jorge (contact).
  4. Pay MRV + service fee.
  5. Payment processed at a Mexican bank.
  6. You receive scanned receipt and then original by mail if needed.
  7. Use the receipt to schedule your ASC & visa interviews.

Mexico Residency & Document Preparation

San Diego Leisure also assists with Temporary and Permanent Mexican Residency applications by preparing paperwork and guiding clients through:

  • Visa application document preparation (for Mexican consulates in the U.S.).
  • Canje process document preparation (exchange of Mexico visa for resident card inside Mexico).
  • Combined preparation for both visa and canje.
  • Renewal document support for Mexican resident cards.

Examples of document services offered:

  • Mexico visa preparation: ~$400
  • Canje: ~$400
  • Visa + Canje: ~$750
  • Renewal paperwork: ~$550
  • Change of status: ~$650

4. I-94 Extension Service

Helps clients obtain a new I-94 extension when needed—important if leaving and re-entering the U.S. Includes round-trip support and stops at Mexican immigration for FMM (tourist card) processing.

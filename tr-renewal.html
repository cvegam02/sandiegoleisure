What is a Mexico temporary residency renewal?

A Mexico temporary residency renewal is the process that extends your current Temporary Resident status for more time in Mexico.

All renewals are handled by the Mexican immigration authority:

Instituto Nacional de Migración (INM)

This process is completed inside Mexico at the corresponding immigration office near your Mexico address, not at a Mexican consulate.

Who must renew?

You must renew if:

  • you already have a Temporary Resident Card, and
  • your card is close to its expiration date.

This renewal process does not apply to Permanent Residents.

When should I start my Mexico residency renewal?

You can begin your Mexico temporary resident card renewal:

  • up to 30 days before your card expires

Starting early helps avoid:

  • Missing the due date
  • last-minute travel problems

If your card has already expired, your case must be reviewed individually by Jorge Gonzalez, Mexico Visa Expert!

Where do I renew my Mexico resident card?

Your Mexico resident card renewal must be filed:

  • inside Mexico, and
  • at the INM office that corresponds to your registered Mexican address.

You cannot renew your card at a consulate.

How long can I renew my Temporary Resident Card for?

Depending on your budget, you may renew for:

  • 1 year
  • 2 years
  • or 3 years

You may hold Temporary Residency for a maximum of four total years.

After that, you automatically qualify to change to Permanent Residency.

2026 government fees for Mexico temporary resident renewal

These are the official government fees for Mexico temporary resident card renewal in 2026.

They are paid directly to the immigration authority in Mexican pesos, with a visa or Mastercard credit card.

Renewal period 2026 government fee:

Temporary Resident - 1 year MXN $11,141

Temporary Resident - 2 years MXN $16,693

Temporary Resident - 3 years MXN $21,142

Temporary Resident - 4 years MXN $25,058

Some applicants may qualify for a reduced government fee (for example, certain family-based cases).

Eligibility must be confirmed before payment.

What documents are normally required?

For most Mexico temporary resident card renewal cases, you will need:

  • your current Temporary Resident Card
  • your passport
  • appointment
  • the renewal application
  • petition letter
  • proof of your Mexican address (when required by your local office). So far, most of the INM Immigration offices do not require this.

Can I travel while my renewal is in process?

If you must leave Mexico while your Mexico residency renewal is being processed, you must request an exit and re-entry permit before you leave. You will be granted 60 days to return and pick up your residency card.

Leaving Mexico without this permit can result in your renewal being canceled and requiring you to start over.

FAQ – Mexico Temporary Resident Card Renewal (2026)

Do Permanent Residents renew their cards?

No. Permanent Residents do not renew their residency cards their status is lifetime.

Can I renew my Temporary Resident Card after it expires? Sometimes yes, but the procedure is more complicated and must be reviewed on a case-by-case basis.

How much is the Mexico residency renewal fee in 2026?

The government fee ranges from MXN $11,141 to MXN $25,058, depending on how many years you renew.

Can I renew my Mexico residency outside Mexico? No. All renewals are completed inside Mexico at the immigration office.

Can I change from Temporary to Permanent during renewal?

Yes, if you qualify. This should be reviewed before submitting your renewal application.

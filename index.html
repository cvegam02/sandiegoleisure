Three Easy Steps: How to apply for Temporary or Permanent Mexico Residency visa in the Mexican Consulates in the U.S.:

  1. Step 1: Mexican Consulate Application

    Apply in person for a TR or PR Mexico Visa at the Mexican Consulate or Embassy in the USA.

  2. Step 2: Border or Airport Verification

    One to 180 days after visa approval, verify it and obtain an entry stamp and an FMM FORM FOR CANJE at the Mexico border or airport.

  3. Step 3: Resident Card Exchange

    Thirty days from verification, exchange the Temporary or Permanent Mexico Visa and the FMM FOR CANJE for the Temporary Resident card or Permanent Resident Card at the closest Mexico INM office where you will live.

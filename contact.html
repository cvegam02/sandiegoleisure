Introduction to San Diego Leisure

San Diego Leisure is a California-based service that helps people with Mexico immigration and travel-related processes. We specialize in consular trip packages to the U.S. Consulate in Tijuana, Mexico, making the process easier, organized, and stress-free.

Our goal is simple: help you understand what to do, prepare correctly, and avoid mistakes that cause delays.

Personalized Guidance You Can Trust

If you are not sure which Mexico residency visa is right for you, we are here to help. Jorge Gonzalez personally explains your options, answers your questions, and makes sure you understand the process before moving forward.

You will never be rushed or left guessing. The goal is clarity, confidence, and a smoother path to Mexico residency.

Our Services

Help With Mexico Residency

In addition to consular trip packages, we assist clients with:

  • Temporary Mexico Residency
  • Permanent Mexico Residency
  • CANJE PROCESS ASSISTANCE
